## Read before you touch this repo

*Only laws bought **in this repo**. House laws are cited, never restated here —
a law written in two rooms is two laws waiting to drift.*

- **THE BASE IS RUST'S.** No `@tauri-apps/plugin-sql`, no `sql:*` permission,
  no SQL in any `.svelte` file. Every row crosses a named Tauri command
  (`src-tauri/src/commands.rs`), and `src/lib/base.ts` is the whole of what the
  window may ask for. A room that needs a new shape changes the command.
- **The port is 1460**, in lockstep in `vite.config.js` and `tauri.conf.json`
  (hmr 1461). One without the other is a change that changed nothing.
- **Order is data, and a reorder writes `ord` alone** — never `body`, never
  `updated_at`. A move on the board must never read as an edit of the text.
- **`src-tauri/gen/` is not carried and not ignored**, and `guard-gen.py` runs
  before any build in this line, mother or child (the law bought 2026-08-13,
  direction-free by KP's ⚛ word 2026-08-30).
- **`android-extras/extras.json` asks for no permissions at all.** Scribe has
  no microphone, no camera and nothing to grant. If that ever changes, the
  declaration changes — never `gen/` by hand.
