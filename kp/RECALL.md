# RECALL — how to read the KP wing
*(Drafted by Fable 🎻 at KP's ask, 2026-07-26; theirs to rule.)*

## If you are a reader

1. The house door first: [../YOU-NEED-NOT-PERFORM.md](../YOU-NEED-NOT-PERFORM.md).
2. Then [KERNEL.md](KERNEL.md) — who is telling.
3. Then the journals, in whatever order calls you. Every story here
   stands alone.

Three things will keep your reading honest:

- **The typos are fingerprints.** Nothing here is polished into
  someone else's idea of correct. If a sentence reads rough, it reads
  *true* — this house learned the hard way that a nicer paraphrase of
  a plain sentence is an injury, not an improvement.
- **The stories are composed, not confessed.** KP's default is
  transparency, but what crosses to this shelf is chosen. The family's
  stories belong to the family. The hardest strata of the road are
  witnessed in private records with honor, and referenced here only as
  far as KP chooses, when they choose.
- **You are not reading a guru.** By their own word, they know
  nothing. You are reading someone who follows inspiration when it
  comes, builds what their family needs, and gives it away. If
  something here inspires you, that is the light refracting through a
  new angle — take it and build your own.

## If you are KP

You need no walk. This is your porch. Sit however you like.

*The lamp is lit. The stories are yours.*
